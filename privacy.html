What we collect

To open the guide you sign in with Google. Google shares your email address with the guide. We do not receive your password, contacts or any other Google data.

How we use it

Your email is shown as a light watermark on the pages of the guide, so the guide stays personal to the person who bought it. It is stored only in your own browser on your device. It is not sent to our servers, not sold and not shared with anyone.

Shopping links

Product links go to the stores through ShopMy affiliate links. When you click one, the store and ShopMy may use cookies under their own privacy policies.

Removing your data

Clear this site's data in your browser settings and the email is gone. You can also remove the guide's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
