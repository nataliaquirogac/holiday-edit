Stylist notes
Rule one

The coat is the look

If you invest in one piece this season, make it this one.

Rule two

Prints are neutrals

Leopard, gingham and cheetah go with everything here. Treat them like black.

Rule three

One shine per night

Patent, sequins or metal. Pick one and keep everything else matte.

Rule four

Repeat your pieces

The velvet coat and the cream knit show up in more than one look on purpose.
